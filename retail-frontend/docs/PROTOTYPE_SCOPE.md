# RetailERP Prototype Scope

## Completed modules

RetailERP covers nine primary business modules and 14 additional feature areas:

1. Demo Centre and guided presentation
2. POS and sales history
3. Customer CRM
4. Product and serial catalogue
5. Inventory stock, ledger and analysis
6. Warehouse receipts, transfers and dispositions
7. Finance, collections and receivables
8. Fulfilment and installation handoff
9. Warranty and service desk

The prototype also includes executive analytics, store comparison, sales and customer analytics, inventory analytics, finance analytics, fulfilment analytics, unified reports, alerts, KPI details, administration screens and general settings.

## Demo-only functionality

- Shared browser-session data
- Fictional ACS Electronics companies, stores, customers, products and serials
- Guided transaction journeys
- Print-friendly invoice and delivery layouts
- CSV report downloads
- Simulated role switching

## Not implemented

- Production backend or Spring Boot services
- PostgreSQL or durable server persistence
- Real authentication and access control
- Tenant isolation
- Payment gateway, bank or UPI settlement
- Live GPS or driver tracking
- Statutory GST filing or e-invoicing
- Real customer communications
- Production backup, monitoring or disaster recovery
